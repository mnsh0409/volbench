# IJF writing register

Apply these rules to every draft; end each pass by listing the 3 weakest passages in the draft and why.

**Claims.** Scale every claim to its evidence. A ranking claim requires DM/MCS support; a "better" without inference becomes "achieved lower CRPS in our sample". Never "novel", "powerful", "state-of-the-art" without definition and citation. Name what does NOT follow from the results before the discussion section ends.

**Tense & voice.** Past tense for what was done ("we evaluated 14 configurations"); present tense for what the software does ("the splitter produces index pairs"). First person plural acceptable; keep it sparse. Active voice by default.

**Numbers.** Every number in prose must be traceable to a results table generated by the pipeline; no retyped values. Report uncertainty or sample size alongside point summaries. Consistent significant figures within a table.

**Citations.** Only cite works with an entry in the project's reading notes; otherwise insert [TODO-VERIFY] and stop. Cite software with version numbers. Competitions and datasets get formal citations, not just URLs.

**Structure.** Every figure and table is referenced and interpreted in the text — no orphan exhibits. Section budgets per the paper outline; flag any section running >20% over. Captions self-contained: a reader must understand the exhibit without the body text.

**Notation.** Match the metrics reference exactly; define every symbol at first use; American spelling throughout.

**Forbidden list.** "significantly" without a test; "clearly/obviously"; "very"; hype adjectives; unexplained acronyms; sentences over ~35 words when two would do.
