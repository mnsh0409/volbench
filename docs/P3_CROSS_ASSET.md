# P3 — cross-asset aggregation, ranks only

The nine equity series are scored against an overnight-plus-range variance target and the two crypto series against 5-minute realized variance, so no loss is pooled or averaged across assets anywhere in this file. Every summary is a rank or a count.

**Reported, not interpreted.** No model is ranked as better, no number is called large or small, and nothing here says which forecast wins. Where a table is ordered it says by what. No line here names an overall best; none can be derived from this file without adding an assumption it does not make.

## Conventions

- **Rank** = position of a model's mean loss among the 13 within one asset, 1 = smallest, on the listwise-complete sample the MCS ran on (docs/P3_MCS.md); ties share the minimum rank.
- **Blocks:** `all` (11 assets), `equity` (CAC, DAX, DIA, HSI, KOSPI, NDX, NKX, SPY, TWSE), `crypto` (BTC-USD, ETH-USD).
- **MCS membership counts:** in how many of the block's assets the model survives at alpha = 0.10 and 0.25, per statistic (docs/P3_MCS.md, headline runs).
- **Kendall's τ** (τ-b) between two rankings of the 13 models within one asset.


**Every QLIKE figure appears twice.** `qlike` is the stored column; `qlike_ex_near_zero` is the same column with the five near-zero target days of docs/P3_ANALYSIS_VALIDITY.md §1.4 set to NaN (two on CAC, two on TWSE, one on NKX — listed in docs/P3_QLIKE_LEVERAGE.md). The two differ only on those three assets; on the other eight they are the same numbers.
