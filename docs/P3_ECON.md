## The backtest

- `position_t = min(target / forecast_vol_t, 2)` with a 10 % annualised target, held into `t+1`, earning the simple return of the stored log return; an unusable forecast sizes to zero (`n_flat`).
- **Costs** 0, 1, 5, 10 bps of turnover `|position_t - position_{t-1}|`, the first day charged against a flat book.
- **Annualisation per asset class:** 252 on the nine equity series, 365 on BTC-USD and ETH-USD (`econ.periods_per_year_for`); the target, the return, the volatility and the Sharpe all scale with it.
- Annualised return is geometric; the Sharpe is the arithmetic mean over the `ddof = 1` standard deviation of per-period net returns, annualised, risk-free rate 0. `gross` is before costs.
- **Sharpe difference.** `dS = S(model) - S(garch11)` on the same origin axis, with a **percentile moving-block bootstrap** (B = 10,000): one index sequence resamples both net-return series together, each resample's Sharpe is its mean over its `ddof = 1` standard deviation, and the 2.5 / 97.5 percentiles of the resampled differences are the interval. Block length: the Politis-White rule's larger value over `r_m - r_b` and `r_m² - r_b²`, rounded up. Seeds are in the manifest. `CI excludes 0` is a mechanical reading of the two bounds.

- Cells whose levered book was wiped out (`ruined`): **0** of 572.

- Intervals excluding zero: **46** of 528 model x asset x cost cells.
